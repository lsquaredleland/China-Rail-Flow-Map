# 5611/5612次列车 — identification

Read 2026-10-22 (index fetches; page crawls noted per source).

## Primary source: Chinese Wikipedia "5611/5612次列车"
URL: https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/5611/5612%E6%AC%A1%E5%88%97%E8%BD%A6 (crawled ~45 days before 2026-10-22, i.e. early Sep 2026; read via browser_open 2026-10-22)

- Class: 普通旅客快车 (conventional passenger express, NO letter prefix). Chengdu Bureau Group (成都局集团), Chongqing passenger depot (重庆客运段). In service since July 1952 (opening of the Chengyu Railway); former numbers 8611/8612.
- It is the ONLY remaining passenger train pair on the old 成渝铁路 (old Chengyu conventional line) — a "公益慢火车" (public-welfare slow train). 25G coaches (YZ25G hard seat, YW25G crew sleeper, XL25G luggage), HXD1C/HXD3C electric locos, Chongqing locomotive depot.
- CURRENT endpoints: 内江站 (Neijiang) ↔ 江津站 (Jiangjin, a suburban district of Chongqing) — entirely on the old 成渝铁路, passenger mileage 219 km. It does NOT serve Chengdu at all, and no longer reaches central Chongqing stations.
- Direction/numbers per article BODY text: 5612 = 江津→内江, 5h54; 5611 = 内江→江津, 6h26. Times: Neijiang dep 10:30 → Jiangjin arr 16:56 (6h26, = 5611); Jiangjin dep 11:40 → Neijiang arr 17:34 (5h54, = 5612).
- ⚠️ Internal inconsistency in the article: the infobox 发车/终到 rows attach the numbers the other way round (labels the 10:30 Neijiang departure "5612次"). The infobox 运行时间 row (6h26 = 5611次, 5h54 = 5612次) and the body text agree with each other and with the arithmetic, so the body assignment is used here. Corroboration: Trip.com travel piece (older, 重庆南 era) also has 5612 running Chongqing South → (westbound, toward Neijiang) and 5611 returning eastbound.
- 22 intermediate stops (Neijiang→Jiangjin order as listed): 椑木镇、双凤驿、迎祥街、隆昌、李市镇、安富镇、广顺场、荣昌、峰高铺、大足、长河碥、双石桥、永川、临江场、柏林、茨坝、朱杨溪、平等、白沙、金刚沱、油溪、古家沱. I.e. it stops at essentially every small station — the opposite of a nonstop.
- Endpoint history (truncations at the Chongqing end, all due to station closures for the 成渝铁路 upgrading project):
  - until 2022-06-19: 重庆站 (Chongqing, Caiyuanba).
  - 2022-06-20: 重庆站 suspended → terminus 重庆南站 (Chongqing South).
  - 2024-06-14: 重庆南站 suspended passenger service → terminus 江津站.
  - 2025-07-09 to 2026-09-02: 江津站 suspended → terminus temporarily 古家沱站 (Gujiatuo), making it CR Chengdu Group's shortest scheduled train.
  - After 2026-09-02 the article (crawled early Sep 2026) again lists 江津 as terminus and 5611/5612 as 当前车次 (current numbers) — i.e. still in the timetable in late 2026, on the restored Neijiang↔Jiangjin section.
